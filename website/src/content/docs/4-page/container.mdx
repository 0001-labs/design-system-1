A responsive vertical container that keeps its content within the available width. On a wide screen it stands centred at fifty squares (1000px); on a smaller one it takes the width there is. Use it for a page's single column when you need no more than that, and [Page](/4-page/page/) for navigation, columns or a reading measure in whole squares.

## Behaviour

The container stacks its default-slot content. At viewport widths above 820px it is centred with a maximum width of 1000px; on smaller viewports it follows the available width. Its children are constrained to the container’s width.

```html
<ds-container>
  <ds-text size="display" literal>A room for reading</ds-text>
  <ds-gap></ds-gap>
  <ds-text literal wrap>Take a seat, choose a page and stay a while.</ds-text>
</ds-container>
```

The children stand one below the other with nothing between them, so put a [Gap](/2-core/gap/) where they need room. The container adds no padding, and its widths are plain pixels: they read the viewport and are not multiplied by the surface scale.

## Accessibility

A container has no role and draws nothing. It adds no landmark, so wrap it in `main` or `section` where the page needs one. The reading order is the order of its children.

## API

The component has a default slot and no public attributes. There is no `size` or `fluid` attribute. It has no parts, events or tokens either. Add `hidden` to hide it.
