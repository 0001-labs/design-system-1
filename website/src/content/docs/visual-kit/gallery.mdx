Compare all 20 device variants and platform windows. Sign in with the owner account to view the licensed artwork.

The gallery sets one short orders table in a window and a phone side by side. Choose the window (Safari, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, a macOS app or Compact Mac), the appearance, the phone's device, network and time, and a browser address; each change applies at once. Under them stand the browser toolbars in light and dark, then every device bezel with its name. Pressing a control in a window's chrome reports its action under the stage and goes no further.

The original artwork is served only through the owner session and is not part of the public package. [VisualKit](/visual-kit/) documents the components themselves.
